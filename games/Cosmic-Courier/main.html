Operations Manual

Desktop Controls

  • W, A, S, D - Thrust / Move
  • Mouse - Aim Camera
  • Space - Boost / Hover up
  • Shift - Speed Burst / Slam
  • E - Pick up / Drop Package
  • ESC / P - Pause
  • R - Respawn at Hangar

Gameplay Mechanics

  • Fly near a package and press E to magnet-grab it.
  • Drop it on glowing Delivery Pads.
  • Don't crash! High-speed impacts damage package integrity.
  • Zero integrity = destroyed package.
  • Watch out for planetary hazards like Wind and Magnets.
